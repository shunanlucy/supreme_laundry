Garment Care Guidelines & Policy

Laundry Best Practices

01

Tackle Stains Immediately

The quicker you treat a stain the better it is. Don't let grease, wine, or oil dry up.

02

Sort Into Three Piles

Always separate light whites, dark colours, and delicate fabrics with embellishments.

03

Add-On Heavy Stain Service

Normal wash cycles might not lift old dried marks. Ask our executive for stain add-on.
